## Full description (max 4,000)

```
Honeyguide is the hive log built for South African beekeeping: Cape and African bees, local forage, local pests and seasons that run the right way round. It works with no signal, it is free, and your records stay private.

LOG AN INSPECTION IN A MINUTE
• A seven-step guided inspection designed for gloved hands: temperament, queen, brood, stores and space, pests and disease, actions and photos
• A health score and trend for every hive, and "same as last time"
• Weather from the apiary's approximate location, with a "good day to inspect" check
• Suspected American foulbrood? A clear isolate-and-report card

WORKS OFFLINE
• Everything saves on your phone first and syncs when you have signal
• Optional free account to back up and use more than one phone

SHARE WITH A PARTNER OR A MENTOR
• Share chosen apiaries by QR code, WhatsApp link or a 6-character code
• Roles: partner, helper or viewer. Cover mode ends a neighbour's access by itself
• Mentor mode: let an experienced beekeeper see one hive and leave comments
• Exact locations stay hidden unless you allow them

QR HIVE LABELS
• Print QR labels for every box on A4 sheets or label-printer rolls
• Scan a label to open the hive or start an inspection

KNOW YOUR FORAGE
• Plants South African beekeepers report: spotted aloe, sweet thorn, fynbos, canola and more
• A bloom calendar and an 8-week outlook for each apiary: flow, dearth or pollen gap
• Bloom Watch: log what is flowering near your hives

FIELD RECORDS
• Swarm traps with check reminders and a catch log
• Weekly weight or lift test with a flow and dearth trend
• Colony autopsy when a colony dies or absconds
• Equipment and cleaning log, with quarantine for boxes from dead-outs

FOR YOUR BEE BUSINESS
• Pollination contracts with spray agreements and invoices
• Harvest log and jar batches with an optional public honey passport
• Bee-removal job book, hive move planner and a theft and incident report pack
• Landowner log with reminders before a permission runs out

LEARN TO KEEP BEES IN SOUTH AFRICA
• Honeyguide Academy: twelve short lessons with quizzes, from registration to your first inspection
• Waggle Dance Lab, Flower Match and Hive Doctor practice cases
• Ranks and seasonal quests that reward calm, well-timed beekeeping
• A season replay card to share your year

SAFETY FIRST
• One-tap emergency numbers and a "read out my position" button
• Night mode (red on black) for moving hives after dark, and a high-contrast Sun mode

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Your records, photos and apiary locations are yours unless you share them
• Photos are cleaned of GPS data
• App lock with a PIN, and an optional Vault that encrypts pins, landowners and money on your phone
• No ads, no trackers, nothing sold
• Export or delete your data any time

Honeyguide gives general guidance, not veterinary or legal advice. It never shows chemical treatment doses. Learn with your local beekeeping club and a mentor too.
```

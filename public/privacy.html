Privacy policy

Honeyguide: Beekeeping SA · Effective 27 October 2026 · Version 1

In short: your beekeeping records belong to you. They stay on your phone unless you sign in to back them up. Nobody else can see them unless you invite them. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics trackers.

Who we are

Honeyguide is published by Jaco Prinsloo, South Africa ("we"). We are the responsible party for the personal information described here under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Contact: jacoprinsloo.me@gmail.com.

We do not collect health information, biometric data, contacts, or information about your race, religion or other special personal information. App lock PINs stay on your phone, stored as a one-way hash.

Who can see your information

  • You. Everything you enter.
  • People you invite to share an apiary, limited by the role you give them. Exact apiary GPS is hidden from them unless you allow it.
  • Nobody else. Our database enforces row-level access rules, so one user cannot read another user's records. We do not read your records except when you ask us for help or when the law requires it.

Bloom Watch sightings may be shown to other beekeepers only as anonymous counts per province and week, and only when at least three sightings exist. They never include your name, apiary or location.

Honey passports are public only if you switch them on for a jar batch. Anyone who scans that jar's QR code then sees the batch name, forage, province, harvest month and batch size, plus your registration number and public contact only if you choose to show them. A passport never shows a location or apiary.

Service providers and where data is stored

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). This is a transfer outside South Africa, which POPIA (section 72) allows where the recipient is bound by protections substantially similar to POPIA. EU data protection law applies to the host. By creating an account you agree to this transfer.
  • Vercel serves the app's web files. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather. When weather is switched on, the app sends it the apiary's position rounded to about 1 km, and nothing else. You can switch weather off in More › Privacy.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database host processes data for us under its data processing agreement, as POPIA (section 21) requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your records while your account exists. When you delete your account, your records and photos are deleted from our database straight away; copies may remain in our host's routine backups for a short period before they are overwritten. Records kept only on your phone stay there until you remove them or uninstall the app.

Your rights and choices

  • Export all your records at any time: More › Your data › Export.
  • Delete your account and cloud data in the app (More › Your data › Delete my account) or on the web at delete-account.html.
  • Correct anything by editing it in the app.
  • Object or complain. Contact us first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

Security

We use access rules on every database table, private photo storage, encrypted connections, and an optional app lock. No system is perfectly secure. If a security compromise affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA (section 22) requires.

Children

Honeyguide is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes, and tell you in the app about important changes.

This policy is written in plain language for a small, free app. It is not legal advice; it will be reviewed before launch.

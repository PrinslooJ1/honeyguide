Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Honeyguide account and everything stored in the cloud for it: apiaries, hives, inspections, photos, bloom sightings and memberships of shared apiaries. It cannot be undone.

Records kept on your phone are not touched. To remove those too, open Honeyguide › More › Your data › Remove all data from this phone, or uninstall the app.

In the app

Open Honeyguide › More › Your data › Delete my account and cloud data.
